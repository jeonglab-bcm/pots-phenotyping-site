POTS deep phenotyping

The literature layer for an OMOP computable phenotype of postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome: a harvested PubMed corpus, a 41-facet vocabulary, and an ontology linking tests, treatments and conditions to six possible causes of POTS, each link backed by a quoted passage.

Draft for review. The ontology and its source excerpts were written by an AI assistant (Claude) and have not yet been reviewed by a clinician.
